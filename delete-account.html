Delete your CryptoSim account

CryptoSim by Ashfaq Khan App studio

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open CryptoSim and sign in.
  2. Go to Home / Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email dr.ashfaqkhan38@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "CryptoSim account deletion". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email and login), username, virtual balance and profit/loss, holdings, trade history, futures positions and security logs. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept

Nothing. No data is kept after deletion.
