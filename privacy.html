Privacy Policy

CryptoSim by Ashfaq Khan App studio · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Simulator notice

CryptoSim is an educational paper-trading simulator. All balances, trades and profits are virtual. No real money or real cryptocurrency is deposited, traded or withdrawn, and nothing in the app is financial advice.

What we collect

  • Email address (to create and secure your account)
  • Username (shown on the public leaderboard)
  • Virtual trades (coin, amount, price, time)
  • Virtual holdings, virtual balance and profit/loss
  • Security events (for example rate-limit or invalid-input flags) to prevent cheating

What we don't collect

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Device ID or advertising ID (the app removes the advertising ID permission)
  • Real money, bank or card details
  • Photos, files, microphone or camera

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are requested as non-personalized. Google may process limited technical data (such as IP address) to deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's policy. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change it at any time in the app under Profile → Ad privacy choices.

How we store it

Account data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) hosted in the EU, encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS/TLS only). Row-Level Security ensures each user can only read and change their own data. On your phone, your session and portfolio cache are kept in encrypted storage. Your email is never shown to other users.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed only by our service providers: Supabase (database and login) and Google AdMob (ads).

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account permanently erases your profile, holdings, trades and security logs from our servers and wipes the local cache.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can access, correct, export or erase your data at any time. Use Delete Account in the app for immediate erasure (see how to delete your account), or email us for any other request. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Market data

Prices and charts come from the public Binance and CoinGecko APIs. No personal data is sent to them.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
